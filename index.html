About the chapel

I've been mildly obsessed with gothic architecture for a while now. Not really sure why. I'm not a religious person but I really like churches. They are like train stations to the afterlife. Bird cages for the holy ghost.

Churches are designed to slow you down and make you look up. They have this really cool, hushed ambient noise. It's nice to walk in and sit down for a bit.

Made this using Threejs and Blender for the most part. The building model is based on this really nice scan of the Ledebour's forest chapel by kubacpetr. It's somewhere in the Czech Republic. I did remodel and texture the whole thing and design the interior. The pulpit is a remesh of this one from the Virtual Museum of Małopolska. Ambient track by SoundsForYou. I can't really tell where I got the trees and plants.

Made by Ricardo Costa in 2023
